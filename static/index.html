How it works

  1. 1 Retrieve relevant research

    Your question is matched against 2,000 PubMed records using keyword and meaning-based search together.

  2. 2 Compare the evidence

    The top-ranked abstracts are read side by side, and the ranking each method produced is kept visible.

  3. 3 Generate a cited summary

    An answer is drafted only from those abstracts, with every claim linked to the record it came from.

Measured, not claimed

Retrieval quality scored against relevance judgments frozen before any tuning. These values are read from the running service, including where the system is weak.
